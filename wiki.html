BANTAY: ALAMAT NG GABI — CODEX

Welcome to the official companion wiki for BANTAY, a zero-asset browser action game rooted in Philippine mythology and Filipino martial arts. Defend the barangay through rotating moonlit stages using five traditional weapons against waves of nocturnal creatures and the moon-devouring Bakunawa.

The 5 Ancestral Weapons

Slot 1 • Doblete

Arnis (Dual Sticks)

Damage: 15 (22 finisher)
Cooldown: 0.17s
Range: 64–76 px
Stun: 0.08s

Twin rattan fighting sticks used in rapid alternating strikes. Every 3rd hit executes a forward-lunging finisher for 1.45× damage.

Tip: Best for building high Combo multipliers and shredding packs of Tiyanak.
Slot 2 • Tirador

Slingshot

Damage: 22 (42 charged)
Cooldown: 0.34s
Range: 480 px
Pierce: 1 (3 charged)

Traditional carved wooden Y-frame slingshot launching blessed stones. Hold Left Click for 0.42s to charge a glowing piercing critical shot.

Tip: Ideal for picking off airborne Manananggal and kiting Kapre from safety.
Slot 3 • Tungkod

Staff

Damage: 26 (35 wave)
Cooldown: 0.42s
Range: 105–127 px
Knockback: 220–297

Long ceremonial hardwood staff with jade ferrule. Sweeps a wide arc and unleashes a 360° Hangin! wind shockwave on every 3rd attack.

Tip: Use when surrounded to push entire waves back and create breathing room.
Slot 4 • Kalasag

Shield

Bash Dmg: 20
Reflect Dmg: 2.2× incoming
Knockback: 340
Stun: 0.75s

Carved wooden Filipino war shield. Hold click to raise a directional ward that blocks hits and reflects projectiles back at enemies; tap click to Shield Bash.

Tip: Reflecting Bakunawa's lunar orbs or Kapre's embers deals huge burst damage!
Slot 5 • Solo Baston

1 Stick (Kamagong)

Damage: 38 (55 vs stunned)
Cooldown: 0.46s
Range: 76 px
Stun: 1.15s

Single dense ironwood fighting stick built for punishing overhead strikes. Inflicts the longest stun in the arsenal and deals +45% guaranteed critical damage to stunned foes.

Tip: Interrupt charging Tikbalang or chain stuns after a Shield Bash.

Mythical Bestiary

Wave 1+ • Swarmer

Tiyanak

Base HP: 34
Speed: 150
Damage: 10
Score: 60

Feral nocturnal creatures from Philippine folklore known for luring travelers into the forest. Fast and relentless in packs.

Counter: Sweep packs away with Staff or melt them with Arnis combos.
Wave 1+ • Stalker

Aswang

Base HP: 65
Speed: 122
Damage: 16
Score: 120

Shapeshifting night predator feared across Visayan and Tagalog legends. Approaches relentlessly with outstretched razor claws.

Counter: Stun-lock with 1 Stick or outrange with charged Slingshot pellets.
Wave 2+ • Ranged Flyer

Manananggal

Base HP: 55
Speed: 95
Damage: 14
Score: 150

A severed upper-torso creature with massive bat wings that hovers at mid-range and spits crimson cursed bolts.

Counter: Snipe with Slingshot or reflect its bolts right back with Kalasag Shield.
Wave 3+ • Charger

Tikbalang

Base HP: 115
Speed: 105 (440 charge)
Damage: 22
Score: 220

Tall horse-headed forest spirit with a fiery mane. Pauses briefly with a dashed telegraph line before charging rapidly across the arena.

Counter: Sidestep with Spacebar dash, or stop its windup cold with 1 Stick.
Wave 4+ • Brute

Kapre

Base HP: 180
Speed: 68
Damage: 26 (16 ember)
Score: 300

Hulking dark tree giant that dwells in balete and acacia trees, smoking a massive cigar and hurling 3-way fiery ember volleys.

Counter: High knockback resistance — reflect its 3-way embers with Shield!
Every 5th Wave • Boss

Bakunawa

Base HP: 850 (scales/wave)
Speed: 115
Damage: 28 (18 orb)
Score: 1,500 + 30 HP Heal

The giant moon-eating sea serpent of Visayan and Bicolano mythology. Its arrival triggers an eclipse (Laho) and unleashes 8-way radial lunar orb rings.

Counter: Reflect lunar orbs with Kalasag Shield, then rush in with Arnis or 1 Stick.

The 5 Rotating Stages

Waves 1, 6, 11...

Kagubatan ng Kawayan

Moonlit Bamboo Grove. Mossy forest floor surrounded by swaying bamboo clusters and glowing golden alitaptap (fireflies).

Waves 2, 7, 12...

Barangay sa Tabing-Ilog

Torchlit Riverside Village. Packed earth courtyard bordered by a flowing moonlit river and four flickering bamboo torches (sulo).

Waves 3, 8, 13...

Lumang Sementeryo

Misty Ancient Ruins. Weathered stone flagstones, mossy pillars, pale violet moonlight, and drifting spectral mist.

Waves 4, 9, 14...

Tuktok ng Bundok

Sacred Mountain Shrine. High basalt terrace featuring an etched 8-ray Philippine sun motif and four glowing cyan spirit braziers.

Waves 5, 10, 15... (Boss Arena)

Lawa ng Bakunawa

Blood-Moon Eclipse Lake. Dark reflective water with expanding crimson ripples, obsidian shore boulders, and an eclipsed blood moon.

Diwata Blessings (Upgrades)

🔥 Offense

Asin at Bawang

+18% damage across all 5 weapons. Inspired by traditional Filipino folk charms used to ward off nocturnal creatures.

🍃 Mobility

Bilis ng Hangin

+14% movement speed and faster stamina recovery — great for frequent dodging and sustained Kalasag blocking.

💚 Vitality

Biyaya ni Bathala

+25 Max HP and immediately restores 45 HP. Essential before Wave 5 Bakunawa encounters.

⚡ Tempo

Kamay ng Kidlat

+15% attack speed for every weapon. Turns Arnis into a blur and lets Staff trigger wind waves much faster.

🌙 Precision

Lakas ng Buwan

+15% Critical Hit chance (1.85× damage). Stacks nicely with high-hit-rate Arnis or charged Slingshot shots.

🛡 Defense & Control

Matibay na Kalasag

+30% Shield reflect damage and +25% stun duration — supercharges both Shield and 1 Stick builds.

Philippine Folklore & Martial Arts Glossary

TermMeaning & Cultural Origin
BantayTagalog for "guardian" or "watchman" — the protector of the barangay.
MandirigmaTraditional Filipino term for a warrior.
PutongTraditional cloth headband worn by pre-colonial warriors; red signified bravery in battle.
Doblete / Solo BastonFilipino Martial Arts (Arnis/Eskrima/Kali) terms for double-stick and single-stick combat styles.
KalasagTraditional carved wooden war shield used across indigenous Philippine warrior cultures.
Tirador / TungkodFilipino terms for a wooden slingshot (tirador) and hardwood walking/fighting staff (tungkod).
Bathala / DiwataBathala is the supreme deity in Tagalog mythology; Diwata are benevolent nature and guardian spirits.
LahoTraditional term for a lunar eclipse, attributed in folklore to the Bakunawa swallowing the moon.
